Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how XYIHE ("we", "us", "our" or "XYIHE"), trading as an independent software studio based at Exeter Science Park, 6 Babbage Way, Exeter, EX5 2FN, United Kingdom, collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information in connection with:

  • Our website at xyihe.com and any sub-domains (this "Website");
  • Our published iOS / iPadOS applications distributed through the Apple App Store, including but not limited to Contract Verify, Film Grade, Practice Time, Side Ledger, Health Ledger, and Maker Stock (together, the "Apps");
  • Any future published applications on the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or any other distribution platform we may adopt;
  • Email, contact, and support correspondence with us;
  • Any related services that reference this Privacy Policy.

We are committed to handling your personal information with care, in accordance with applicable laws including the United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR"), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation ("EU GDPR" 2016/679), the Data Protection Act 2018, the Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA ("CCPA/CPRA"), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA"), and the United Kingdom Age-Appropriate Design Code (ICO), and any other applicable privacy or data protection law.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of the Website and the Apps. Where required by law, you will be asked to provide affirmative consent before any optional data processing takes place.

1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, the following definitions apply:

  • "Personal data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or erasure.
  • "User" or "you" means any natural person who installs, uses, or interacts with the Apps or visits the Website.
  • "Device" means the iPhone, iPad, iPod touch, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch, Android device, or any other compatible hardware on which the Apps are installed.
  • "Child" or "Children" means, depending on jurisdiction, anyone under the age of 13 (United States, COPPA), under 16 (United Kingdom and most of the European Economic Area), or under the age otherwise defined as a minor by the law of the user's habitual residence.
  • "SDK" means a third-party software development kit that may collect or process information from the Apps.
  • "Advertising" means any messaging, banner, video, or other ad unit served through the Apps or the Website, including paid promotions, native content recommendations, and sponsored placements.
  • "Sensitive personal information" has the meaning given under the CCPA/CPRA and includes precise geolocation, race, religion, health, sexual orientation, genetic or biometric data.

2. Data we collect

We believe in collecting the minimum amount of personal data necessary to provide our services. We collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Information you provide voluntarily

  • Account information. The Apps do not require account registration. If you voluntarily contact us by email or via our contact form, we collect your name, email address, organisation, and the content of your message.
  • Support correspondence. Any information you share when writing to contact@xyihe.com, support@xyihe.com, or privacy@xyihe.com for support requests, including diagnostic data you choose to share.
  • Newsletter subscription. If you subscribe to our quarterly mailing list, we collect your email address. You may unsubscribe at any time via the link included in any email or by writing to contact@xyihe.com.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Website analytics. Anonymous, aggregated analytics about how visitors arrive at and use our Website. We use first-party, cookie-free analytics where technically possible.
  • Diagnostics. Standard Apple diagnostic data sent to Apple under their separate privacy policy when you install the Apps. We do not collect any additional device-level telemetry beyond what is necessary to provide the Apps' core functionality.
  • Crash reports. We do not collect or transmit personally identifiable crash reports. If we ever do, you will be asked for explicit consent at the time of collection. We may use Apple's built-in de-identified crash reports, which contain no personal information.

2.3 Information collected by the Apps

  • Content you create in the Apps. The data you create in each App — for example, contract annotations, photo edits, practice logs, side-income entries, health journal entries, inventory SKUs — is stored locally on your Device unless you initiate an explicit export action.
  • User-initiated exports. If you choose to export a PDF, CSV, or other file, the export happens locally on your Device. We do not receive a copy.
  • In-app purchases. App Store transactions are processed exclusively by Apple. We do not receive your credit card number, billing address, or any financial information. Apple's privacy practices apply.

2.4 Information we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect: precise geolocation, contact lists, calendar entries, photos for unrelated purposes, microphone audio outside the explicit feature context, biometric data, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, government identifiers, online identifiers used for cross-site tracking, or any category of sensitive personal information not relevant to the operation of the Apps.

3. How we use data

We use the limited personal data we collect only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries. When you email us or use our contact form, we use the information you provide to respond. We retain correspondence only as long as needed.
  • To deliver the Apps. To allow the Apps to operate on your Device, including storing the data you create locally and rendering the interfaces.
  • To deliver advertising. Where an App displays advertising (described below in §6 — Advertising), we and our advertising partners use limited device-level identifiers to select, deliver, and measure ads.
  • To deliver updates. To push version updates and security patches to your Device through the App Store.
  • To comply with the law. To respond to lawful requests from regulators, courts, or law-enforcement bodies of competent jurisdiction.
  • To protect our rights. To investigate suspected violations of our Terms of Service, or to defend legal claims where appropriate.

We do not use personal data for automated decision-making that produces legal effects or significantly affects you, profiling for credit, profiling for employment, or any form of automated profiling that is incompatible with the principles of this Privacy Policy.

4. Legal basis for processing (UK / EU GDPR)

If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, our legal bases under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR for processing personal data are:

  • Performance of a contract. When you contact us to enquire about a partnership, sign a deal, or use a paid feature of an App, processing is necessary for the contract or in order to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract.
  • Legitimate interests. For limited, proportionate activities such as securing the Website, preventing fraud, and responding to enquiries from prospective partners. We conduct a Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA) where required.
  • Consent. Where we ask for your explicit consent — for example, before sending marketing emails, before storing non-essential cookies, or before enabling optional analytics.
  • Legal obligation. Where we are required by law to retain certain records or disclose them to authorities.

Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal. To withdraw consent, write to privacy@xyihe.com.

Our Apps are published in accordance with the policies of their distribution platforms. We comply in full with:

  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines (current version 5 onwards), including specifically §1 (Objectionable Content), §2 (Functionality), §3 (Metadata), §4 (Design), §5 (Legal). We maintain up-to-date Privacy Manifests for every published App as required by Apple's privacy nutrition labels and Required Reason API documentation (see Apple's Privacy Manifest documentation).
  • Apple's User Privacy and Data Use policy. Where a permission such as User Tracking is requested, the ATT prompt is shown prior to any data collection and on a per-Apps basis.
  • Apple's Sensitive Content Requirements for any App targeting Health, Wellness, or Children.
  • Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data policy and Families policy. We file a complete Data Safety form for each App, accurately declaring every category of data collected or shared.
  • Google Play's "Designed for Families" requirements, where applicable.
  • Apple's and Google's policies on advertising identifiers (IDFA / Google Advertising ID). We support Limited Ad Tracking (LAT) by default in every App and respect user opt-outs.
  • iOS Privacy Manifest declarations for the following Required Reason API categories: UserDefaults, FileTimestamp, SystemBootTime, DiskSpace, ActiveKeyboard, Display, ProcessInfo.

5.1 Privacy nutrition labels

Each of our Apps declares, accurately, on the App Store product page, the categories of data it does and does not collect. Where the App collects no data, the nutrition label will read "Data Not Collected". Where advertising is shown (see §6), the label reflects the advertising requirements of Apple's nutrition label.

All paid transactions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), under their respective privacy policies. We do not receive your payment instrument. We receive only de-identified transaction identifiers, the tier purchased, and country of purchase, for the purpose of unlocking features and providing support.

6. Advertising & Ad SDK disclosures (ad-monetisation)

Several of our Apps show advertising. When an App displays advertising, the following conditions apply:

  • User consent. For Apps using any tracking-related identifiers (Apple's IDFA, Google's GAID), we ask for explicit consent via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt on iOS, or via a granular consent dialog on Android prior to any data being shared with the advertising partner. Limited Ad Tracking (LAT) is honoured by default in every App.
  • Age gating. We do not show personalised advertising to anyone under the age of consent in their jurisdiction (13 in the US, 16 in the UK and most of the EEA). For users below these thresholds, only contextual (non-personalised) advertising is shown. See §9 — Children's Privacy.
  • Data minimisation. We pass only the minimum set of signals required for ad selection, delivery, and measurement. Where possible, we pass no IDFA / GAID at all.
  • Frequency capping & rotation. We respect user-level frequency caps and provide an in-app "Ad settings" panel where supported by the SDK.
  • Reward integrity. For rewarded video ads, we authenticate reward issuance server-side to prevent fraud; rewards are issued immediately on ad completion.
  • Click and conversion events. We send the minimum click/impression event required for billing; we do not send raw user identifiers.
  • No sale of personal data. Under CCPA/CPRA, the use of advertising identifiers may constitute a "sale" or "share" of personal information. We have implemented opt-out signals (Global Privacy Control) and a Do-Not-Sell-or-Share toggle in our advertising partner integrations where required.
  • European users. For users in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, and Switzerland, we serve advertising only through partners that participate in the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF v2.2). We use a certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) to obtain and signal TCF consents. We honour user opt-outs from personalised advertising on a per-request basis.
  • Brazilian users. For users in Brazil, we comply with the LGPD. We treat advertising identifiers as personal data and we honour user opt-outs from consent withdrawal.
  • Children's apps. If any App is classified as primarily targeting children, we do not use personalised advertising and we do not use any SDK that collects personal data from children. Where advertising is served in such Apps, it is contextual only.

Our Apps integrate the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK and may integrate with other advertising platforms for back-fill or campaign-specific reasons. The current list of advertising partners we use is given in §8 — Ad Platforms List.

We never share the content you create inside an App — contracts, photo edits, practice logs, side-income entries, health journal entries, inventory SKUs — with any advertising partner, regardless of consent.

7. Ad formats we use

Depending on the App and your consent, you may see the following standard ad formats. Each format obeys our stated privacy controls.

7.1 Splash (app-open) ads

A full-screen ad shown at app launch, immediately when the App becomes active. Splash ads in our Apps are dismissible after a brief countdown, and can be muted via the device's mute switch. They do not require any consent beyond standard ad display consent.

7.2 Rewarded video ads

Full-screen video ads that a user opts into, usually in exchange for an in-app reward (extra life, premium content, etc.). The user must affirmatively tap a "Watch ad" button. Reward issuance is verified server-side where supported. Skip is permitted at any time after the first 5 seconds.

7.3 Interstitial ads

Full-screen ads shown at natural transition points inside the App (for example, between major actions or sections of a tutorial). They include a clear close button, never appear during active user input, and we implement a session-based frequency cap.

7.4 Banner ads

Small rectangular ad units displayed at the top or bottom of a screen. They are non-intrusive, do not cover user content, and never cover navigation. We do not run expandable, push-down, or sticky banner formats that interfere with the rest of the UI.

7.5 Native / in-feed ads (where supported)

Ads visually matched to surrounding content, labelled clearly with an "Ad" or "Sponsored" marker. We do not permit native ads to be confused with editorial listings in any of our Apps.

7.6 MREC and medium rectangle

Medium rectangular ad units (300×250 or 728×90), typically shown at content boundaries. They are dismissible at any time and do not auto-refresh faster than once every 30 seconds.

If you wish to disable personalised advertising while continuing to use the Apps, open the in-app Settings → Privacy → Advertising panel and toggle "Personalised ads" off. Contextual ads will continue to show.

8.2 Advertising identifiers used

We work with the following identifiers, only after the appropriate consent has been obtained:

  • Apple IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) — used only when the user has granted ATT permission, and only to send to SDKs that the user has consented to.
  • Apple LAT (Limited Ad Tracking) — by default on.
  • Google GAID (Google Advertising ID) — used only when the user has granted the equivalent of ATT on Android.
  • SKAdNetwork — used by Apple for postbacks when IDFA is unavailable; we configure our postback schema per App, attributing only when consented.
  • App set ID (Android) — used in place of GAID on newer Android releases, subject to opt-out.
  • Vendor ID (IDFV) — limited, internal use only; not shared with advertising partners.

8.3 Children-directed considerations

For any App that we classify as directed to children (under 13 in the US, under 16 in the EEA / UK), we configure all advertising integrations as child-directed mode across every partner. This means: no personalised advertising, no remarketing, no audience-based delivery, no data collected that is not strictly necessary to serve a contextual ad and log an aggregated impression count. Where a partner does not support child-directed compliance, we disable that integration entirely.

10. Cookies & similar technologies

10.1 Our Website

Our Website uses a minimum-cookie approach. The cookie categories we use are:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for the Website to function. These include session cookies, security tokens, and language preferences. They do not require consent under UK PECR or EU ePrivacy.
  • Functional cookies — used to remember preferences (e.g., dark / light mode). Off by default; you opt in by interacting with the relevant UI.
  • Analytics cookies — we use a privacy-preserving analytics provider (Plausible or a self-hosted equivalent) which does not set tracking cookies and does not collect personal data. Where we use any traditional analytics SDK that does set cookies (e.g., Google Analytics 4 only as a last resort and behind consent), we obtain PECR consent prior to loading.
  • Marketing cookies — we do not use marketing cookies on this Website for the purposes of personalised advertising.

You may withdraw consent for non-essential cookies at any time by clearing your browser's cookies or by contacting us at privacy@xyihe.com.

10.2 Mobile device identifiers

Cookies do not exist in the same form on iOS and Android. Instead, the equivalent device-level identifiers (IDFA, GAID, App Set ID) are described in §8. The same consent rules apply: strictly necessary use is permitted; everything else requires user consent.

10.3 Global Privacy Control (GPC)

If your browser or device sends a Global Privacy Control header, we honour it as a valid opt-out of "sale or share" under the CCPA/CPRA.

11. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the service or comply with legal obligations.

  • Content you create in the Apps. Retained on your Device until you delete it, uninstall the App, or clear the App's data via your device settings.
  • Support correspondence. Retained for 24 months from the last interaction, unless needed for ongoing enquiries or legal retention obligations.
  • Server logs (Website). Retained for 30 days in raw form and 6 months in aggregated, anonymised form for security analysis.
  • Newsletter subscriptions. Retained until you unsubscribe.
  • Records subject to legal retention (e.g., invoicing, tax). Retained for the minimum retention period required by the applicable law, typically 6 – 7 years.

12. Your privacy rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data, regardless of where you live:

  • Right of access. To request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification. To ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"). To ask us to delete your personal data, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to restrict processing. To ask us to suspend processing of your data while a complaint is investigated.
  • Right to data portability. To receive a portable copy of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object. To object to processing based on legitimate interest, including profiling.
  • Right to withdraw consent. At any time, where consent is the legal basis.
  • Right to opt out of "sale or share" under CCPA/CPRA, including the right to opt out of "targeted advertising", "profiling", and "automated decision-making".
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not deny service, charge different prices, or provide a lower level of service because you exercised a privacy right.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. For UK residents: the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. For EEA residents: your national data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, write to privacy@xyihe.com. We respond within 30 days, or sooner where required. We may need to verify your identity before actioning a request.

13. Children's privacy & age

We are deeply committed to protecting children's privacy. The protections we apply depend on the age and jurisdiction of the user.

13.1 Age ranges under law

  • United States (COPPA): "Child" means under 13.
  • United Kingdom (Age-Appropriate Design Code, AADC): "Child" means under 18. Ofcom's Children's Code extends privacy protections to anyone under 18; the Information Commissioner's Office treats "children" as under 13 for consent.
  • European Economic Area (GDPR): "Child" means under 16, but member states may lower this to 13.
  • Brazil (LGPD): "Child" means under 12.
  • China (PIPL): "Minor" means under 14.
  • Australia (Privacy Act): "Child" means under 16.
  • India (DPDP Act 2023): "Child" means under 18.
  • Canada (PIPEDA): "Child" means under 13, with provincial variations.

13.2 Our commitments

  • No targeted advertising to children. We do not serve personalised advertising, retargeting, or behavioural advertising to anyone under the age of consent in their jurisdiction. Contextual advertising only.
  • No data profiles. We do not build user profiles of children.
  • No behavioural tracking. We do not engage in behavioural tracking of children within our Apps or Website.
  • No access to contact lists. Our Apps do not access the address book, calendar, or contacts library of any Device.
  • No precise geolocation. Our Apps never request precise location from a child.
  • No group audio/video chat with strangers — we do not offer such features in any App.
  • No data sharing with third parties for their own purposes. Child data is not passed to third parties for any reason other than operating the App.
  • Plain language. Every privacy disclosure in the App's UI uses language a child can understand.
  • Sticky defaults. All privacy-preserving toggles (e.g., "Don't share with ad partners") are on by default for child accounts.
  • High-privacy default for any user who has not yet declared their age. We treat them as under the lowest age of consent until verified.
  • No nudging children to weaken privacy settings or hand over more data.

13.3 Age verification

Where it is not clear whether a user is an adult or a child, we ask for age confirmation. Our verification methods include:

  • In-app age gate prompt on first launch, with a "Child", "Teen" or "Adult" choice;
  • Account-based verification, where an account exists (e.g., Sign In With Apple's age band);
  • App Store / Play Store age rating metadata, used as a default.

13.4 Parental controls and erasure

Parents and guardians may, at any time, request that we:

  • Provide a copy of any personal data associated with a child under their care;
  • Delete any personal data associated with a child under their care;
  • Refuse to permit further collection of personal data associated with a child under their care.

To make such a request, write to privacy@xyihe.com from a verifiable email address. We respond within 30 days. We accept signed letters of authorisation sent by post to our registered office at Exeter Science Park, 6 Babbage Way, Exeter EX5 2FN, United Kingdom.
