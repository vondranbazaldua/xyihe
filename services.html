Services & Studio

A studio of six apps,
one philosophy.

We design and ship narrow, considered applications on the App Store, and partner with a small number of teams to do the same. Every product is built on the same foundations: privacy by default, local-by-design, and uncompromising craft.

Service 01 — Published Apps

A focused App Store matrix.

Our published titles cover six distinct personal-workflow categories. Each app is engineered around the principle of data never leaves your device unless it must.

What unites them is technical discipline: lean native code, no accounts, no telemetry of personal data, and an interface that gets out of the way as quickly as possible.

Contract Verify

Lean local contract verifier.

Film Grade

Film-grain colour engine.

Practice Time

Practice timer & repertoire log.

Side Ledger

Side-income analytics.

Health Ledger

Private sleep/mood journal.

Maker Stock

Workshop inventory tracker.

Service 02 — Programmatic Audio & Visual Systems

Procedural engines for sound, image and motion.

We design programmatic audio synthesis pipelines and immersive visual interaction systems for teams who need variety, organicity and depth without storing every variation.

We focus on three properties: unlimited variation, deterministic reproducibility, and latency tight enough to feel handmade.

Procedural audio Generative visuals Real-time grading Motion design Live prototyping
Service 03 — Privacy Engineering

Local-first by engineering default.

We help product teams move from cloud-centric to local-first architectures. We design on-device inference pipelines, write privacy manifest documentation suitable for App Store review and Google Play Data safety, audit network traffic in existing apps, and advise on consent UX for child-directed and family audiences.

Service 04 — Interaction & Visual Design

Quiet, deliberate interfaces.

We build interactive prototypes and design systems that combine elegant minimal aesthetics with deeply considered micro-interactions. From tone and motion through to typography and iconography, we produce assets that age well, render fast, and stay calm on the user's nerves.

Service 05 — Custom Partnerships

A small number of engagements a year.

We accept a limited number of custom product partnerships each year. Our process is compact, candid and design-led. Engagements typically span 8 – 16 weeks and result in a shipped first release on the App Store, Play Store, or the open web.

Discovery

One 60-minute call to understand the problem space.

Proposal

Written scope, milestones, deliverables and budget.

Build

Weekly build notes, shared staging, paired review.

Ship

App Store / Play launch, support handover.
