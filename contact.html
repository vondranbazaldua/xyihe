Our home

Exeter Science Park.

We are based at Exeter Science Park in Devon, on the south-west coast of the United Kingdom. The park is one of the country's leading research campuses, and a good place to write serious software in a calm, well-lit room.

We are an independent studio with no remote-only team. Working from the same room, in the same building, helps us write the kind of code that lasts.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you take on small projects?

We take on between two and four partner engagements a year. Small projects (under four weeks) are difficult for us to schedule; we are best at products that take 8 – 16 weeks of focused work.

Can you build a clone of X?

We do not build direct clones. We do take on problems that often resemble problems others have faced — but the research and the product direction are our own.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, after we have agreed on the engagement scope. We can sign your mutual NDA, or send ours. We are happy working under either.

Do you work with investors?

We do not take outside investment. Our partnerships are typically revenue-share, license, or fee-for-build. We do not trade equity for engineering hours.

Where is the data my app handles?

Personal data never leaves the user's device without their explicit consent. Telemetry from this website is minimal and listed in our Privacy Policy. We never sell user data.
