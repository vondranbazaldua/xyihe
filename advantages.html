Why XYIHE

Six principles
we refuse to compromise.

We are a research and product team based at Exeter Science Park. Our core members have spent over a decade shipping tools in programmatic audio synthesis, visual interaction design, privacy engineering and product innovation.

Principle 01
01

Programmatic systems, not preset surfaces.

Most products are built as fixed surfaces. We work differently.

We deep-invest in programmatic generation technology — systems where every interaction is rendered live, where variety emerges from the engine instead of being authored by hand, and where every user feels like a unique participant.

We push past traditional fixed-mode limitations to deliver infinite variation, organic dynamism, and quietly personalised interaction.

Principle 02
02

A decade of cross-disciplinary craft.

Our core members have spent over ten years building software professionally. We have shipped audio engines, design systems, and privacy frameworks.

The team covers programmatic audio synthesis, visual interaction design, privacy security technology, and product innovation in depth.

This is what we mean by technology serving experience. We treat every project as a craft problem, not a deliverable.

Principle 03
03

Privacy you can audit, not just promise.

Our position is simple: personal data never leaves the device unless the product literally cannot function otherwise, and even then it does so with the user's clear, explicit consent.

No cloud sync of personal data. No third-party tracking. No background telemetry that we could not read ourselves.

We refuse to build features that depend on harvesting user data, and we refuse partners that ask us to. This is non-negotiable.

Network call manifests

Every outbound call documented in our public Privacy Manifests.

On-device by default

Personal data is processed and stored on the user's device. The cloud is for binary distributions.

SDK audit & disclosure

We audit every third-party SDK before integration and disclose each in the App Privacy label.

Explicit consent patterns

Permissions requested at the moment of use, with a plain-language explanation of why.

Principle 04
04

Elegant minimalism with quiet depth.

We design for the long quiet hours of focused work. Our references draw from minimalist aesthetics, Japanese restraint, mid-century Swiss typography, and the tactile material of physical craft.

We believe that minimalism and depth are not opposites. The cleanest interfaces are the hardest ones to build.

We refuse redundant design. We refuse feature creep. The room is calm; the work is real.

Principle 05
05

Technology that respects the user.

We use technology because we love it — not because it is fashionable. We use programmatic generation to give users experiences that feel quietly personal.

Our products celebrate user autonomy: clear export, clear delete, clear ownership of every byte. We aim for products that make a small footprint, last a long time, and never grow embarrassing at scale.

Made in the United Kingdom

Exeter Science Park is our home. We operate under UK and EU law by default, including GDPR, the UK Age-Appropriate Design Code, and the Consumer Rights Act.

Principle 06
06

Independent, accountable, and here to stay.

XYIHE is an independent studio. We have no outside investors, no growth team, no engagement metrics we are trying to game.

That independence is what lets us say the things we say in this document. It is what lets us invest in the long quiet work — the kinds of products that take a year to feel right and a decade to age well. It is what lets us stay.
